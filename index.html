How Meal Monster works

  1. Describe it.Type or say one food or a whole meal in plain words, like "chicken, rice and a salad". No searching a database for every ingredient.
  2. Check the estimate.Get calories, protein, carbs and fat. Edit the food, servings or numbers any time. Undo is there if you need it.
  3. See your day add up.Your daily totals show against your calorie and macro targets, with Day, Week, Month, six-month and Year views of what you log.

Four ways to log food

TextDescribe one food or a full meal in your own words.
VoiceSay what you ate. It's transcribed and logged like text.
PhotoSnap or pick a photo of your food. It estimates what's visible, so check portions.
BarcodeScan supported packaged foods when product data is available. Coverage varies.

Built around your goal

Choose Lose weight, Gain weight, Maintain my weight or Eat healthier. Meal Monster estimates starting calorie and macro targets from your goal, body and activity, and you can adjust them anytime. Targets are estimates, not a meal plan or a medical prescription.

Accountability without oversharing

Invite a trusted person through Friends. You choose, per friend, whether they can see today's calories, your base daily goal, your streak and your equipped skin. They never see your meals, food names, macros, weight, workouts or Apple Health data. A Supporter can join free to cheer you on, with no food tracking of their own.

A companion that makes it stick

Meal Monster is the character that keeps you company while you log. Earn collectible skins by logging consistently, and set the reminders you actually want. The goal is fewer minutes in the app and steadier progress outside it.

Privacy and AI, in plain words

Your meal text, voice and photos go to OpenAI to create an estimate only after you give AI data consent, and you can turn it off. Apple Health is optional and read-only. Read the privacy policy and the consumer health data policy.

Questions

What is Meal Monster?

An iPhone food tracker for adults. Describe what you ate in plain words, by voice, with a food photo, or with a supported barcode, and get an editable estimate of calories, protein, carbs and fat. Then see how your day adds up against your goals.

How do I log a meal?

Type or say it, like "chicken, rice and a salad", snap a photo, or scan a supported barcode. You can edit the food, servings, calories and macros, and Undo is available.

How accurate are the estimates?

They're estimates, not exact measurements. Photos can't guarantee every ingredient or portion, and barcode coverage varies. Everything is editable, and if the app can't make a credible estimate, it asks you to clarify.

Can it help me gain weight, lose weight or maintain?

You pick Lose weight, Gain weight, Maintain my weight or Eat healthier, and the app estimates targets you can adjust. It shows how your logged food adds up against them. It isn't a meal plan, and results aren't guaranteed.

Does it use AI? Is my data shared?

Meal text, voice and photos are sent to OpenAI only after you give AI data consent, which you can revoke. AI usage limits apply. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does it connect to Apple Health?

Optionally. It can read steps and workout or activity information. It never writes to Apple Health.

What can friends see?

Only what you allow, per friend: today's calories, your base daily goal, display name, equipped skin and streak. Never your meals, food names, macros, weight, workouts or health data.

Is it free?

Food tracking is part of Meal Monster Pro, a subscription. Supporter mode is free. Current prices and any trial are shown in the App Store.

Is this medical advice?

No. Meal Monster is a food tracker, not medical nutrition care, a diagnosis tool or eating-disorder treatment, and it doesn't replace a qualified professional.
