How this list works

Questions

What food has the most protein per 100 calories?

On this list, shrimp is first at 24.2 g of protein per 100 calories, then tuna (22 g) and cod (21.7 g).

What does protein per 100 calories mean?

It is the grams of protein you get for every 100 calories of a food. It lets a small food and a big one be compared fairly. It is a number, not a verdict on the food.

Why is a food missing from the list?

A food needs at least 5 g of protein in a normal serving to make this list. Every food we cover, 137 in all, is on the protein in foods page.

Where do the numbers come from?

USDA FoodData Central. Each food page shows the exact USDA entry and its FDC ID.
