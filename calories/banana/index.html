How many calories are in a banana?

One medium banana has about 105 calories and 1.3 g of protein.

Calories and protein

AmountCaloriesProtein
medium banana1051.3 g
3 oz (85 g)760.9 g

Source: USDA FoodData Central, Bananas, raw. Brands and cooking change the numbers a little.
