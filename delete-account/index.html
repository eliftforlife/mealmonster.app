Account & privacy

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Meal Monster account and associated app data from inside the app.

In the Meal Monster app

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Under Account & privacy, choose Delete account.
  3. Review the information shown and follow the confirmation steps.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel the subscription through the store account used for purchase.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app, email support from the address connected to your account. We may need to verify account ownership before completing a request.
