What we measure

First-result food coverage
Which expected foods appear before corrections. Errors stay in the denominator.
Correction burden
Repair actions and corrected fields, with navigation and mandatory confirmations counted separately.
Completion and time
The share of attempted meals completed, alongside median completion time and variability. Fast failures do not win.
Uncertainty and exclusions
Whether unclear portions remain explicit and whether ingredients the user excluded are mistakenly added.

Conditions before publishing results

  • Record public app version, device, OS, access tier, input method, date, and evidence.
  • Use the same meals and preselected sample across apps; retain failures and repeated-run variability.
  • Review first-result coverage separately from the final corrected log.
  • Keep unsupported methods and paywalls separate from failed attempts.
  • Report nutrition error only after independent quantity and nutrient references exist.

Earlier work and our scope

Logging-effort research already exists. MacroFactor described comparing 20 food loggers in 2022, and MyNetDiary publishes a 127-entry comparison. Our proposed contribution is a reusable corpus emphasizing food omissions, correction effort, ambiguity, and repeated runs. It is a testable contribution, not a claim of being first.

Current machine-readable results contain no measured runs yet. Corpus JSON is available under CC BY 4.0 with its version and checksum.
