How much protein is in beer?

12 oz can has about 1.6 g of protein and 153 calories.

Protein per 100 calories

Beer has about 1.1 g of protein per 100 calories. One 12 oz can has under 5 g, so it isn't on our high-protein foods list, which needs at least 5 g per serving. See how much protein you need a day. More: calories in beer.
