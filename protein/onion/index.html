How much protein is in an onion?

One medium onion has about 1.2 g of protein and 44 calories.

Source: USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, FDC ID 170000): Onions, raw. Brands and cooking change the numbers a little.

Protein per 100 calories

An onion has about 2.8 g of protein per 100 calories. One medium onion has under 5 g, so it isn't on our high-protein foods list, which needs at least 5 g per serving. See how much protein you need a day. More: calories in onion.
