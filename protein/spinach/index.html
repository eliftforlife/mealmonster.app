How much protein is in spinach?

One cup, raw has about 0.9 g of protein and 7 calories.

Protein per 100 calories

Spinach has about 12.4 g of protein per 100 calories. One cup, raw has under 5 g, so it isn't on our high-protein foods list, which needs at least 5 g per serving. See how much protein you need a day. More: calories in spinach.
