How much protein is in popcorn?

One cup, air-popped has about 1 g of protein and 31 calories.

Protein per 100 calories

Popcorn has about 3.3 g of protein per 100 calories. One cup, air-popped has under 5 g, so it isn't on our high-protein foods list, which needs at least 5 g per serving. See how much protein you need a day. More: calories in popcorn.
