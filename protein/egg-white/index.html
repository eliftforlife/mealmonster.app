How much protein is in an egg white?

One large egg white has about 3.6 g of protein and 17 calories.

Protein and calories

Source: USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, FDC ID 172183): Egg, white, raw, fresh. Brands and cooking change the numbers a little.

Protein per 100 calories

An egg white has about 21 g of protein per 100 calories. One large egg white has under 5 g, so it isn't on our high-protein foods list, which needs at least 5 g per serving. See how much protein you need a day. More: calories in egg white.
