How much protein is in a peach?

One medium peach has about 1.4 g of protein and 58 calories.

Source: USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, FDC ID 169928): Peaches, yellow, raw. Brands and cooking change the numbers a little.

Protein per 100 calories

A peach has about 2.3 g of protein per 100 calories. One medium peach has under 5 g, so it isn't on our high-protein foods list, which needs at least 5 g per serving. See how much protein you need a day. More: calories in peach.
