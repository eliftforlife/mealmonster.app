How much protein is in a rice cake?

One rice cake has about 0.7 g of protein and 35 calories.

Protein per 100 calories

A rice cake has about 2.1 g of protein per 100 calories. One rice cake has under 5 g, so it isn't on our high-protein foods list, which needs at least 5 g per serving. See how much protein you need a day. More: calories in rice cake.
