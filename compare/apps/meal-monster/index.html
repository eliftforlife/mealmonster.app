When to consider it

Consider it if conversational meal logging and a companion with earned skins appeal to you. The separate Supporter mode is for supporting friends, not free personal food logging.

What to verify before switching

Personal logging requires Pro. Independent correction-burden and timing results have not been collected. Apple Health access is read-only.

Plans and price

Pro is required for personal food logging. Check current offers and trial eligibility in the App Store.

The store download price is Free. That describes installation, not the cost of personal tracking or AI features.
