When to consider it

Consider it if adaptive targets are central to your tracking. Its AI Describe documentation permits reviewing and editing selected foods and quantities.

What to verify before switching

There is no permanent free subscription tier. Speed superiority claims on the vendor site are vendor claims, not results from this project.

Plans and price

The US store description lists $11.99/month, $47.99/6 months, or $71.99/year, checked October 26, 2026. Verify checkout and trial eligibility.

The store download price is Free. That describes installation, not the cost of personal tracking or AI features.
