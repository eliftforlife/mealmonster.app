When to consider it

Consider its photo workflow and typed Describe option for complex foods. The official guide also documents a Fix Results correction control.

What to verify before switching

The store description explicitly requires a subscription for food-scanning analysis. Plan access for the other methods and arbitrary voice descriptions still need verification. PDF export is documented; CSV export is not established.

Plans and price

Food-scanning analysis results require a subscription; current offer prices are unverified.

The store download price is Free. That describes installation, not the cost of personal tracking or AI features.
