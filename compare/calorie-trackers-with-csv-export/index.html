Calorie trackers with advertised spreadsheet export

A food log is easier to keep when you can take it with you. These source records explicitly mention CSV, XLS, Excel, or Google Sheets. Apple Health integration or PDF export alone does not prove a spreadsheet food-log export.

Who makes this resource? Meal Monster publishes it and sells one of the apps listed. Features below are advertised by vendors, checked 2026-10-26. Hands-on speed and correction results are pending. An unverified field is not a missing feature.

What to check on your phone

  • Check whether export includes individual foods or only daily totals.
  • Verify date ranges, units, time zones, and nutrient columns.
  • Check whether export requires a paid plan.
  • Test a small export before relying on it for long-term records.
