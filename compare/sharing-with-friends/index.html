How this comparison was made

We reviewed publisher feature pages, help pages, privacy descriptions, and seven US Apple listings on 2026-10-27. There are 10 selected apps and 60 field records: 25 documented yes, 7 documented no, and 28 not established. This is a selected sample, not a census of every tracker.

We did not install these apps for sharing tests, purchase subscriptions, test permission enforcement, or grade privacy/security. Policies describe intended scope; they do not independently prove implementation. The feature filters group records by source coverage without giving an overall score.

Widget availability can change between a website and store description. Daypair’s store advertises status-only widgets while its homepage labels the section “In the next update”; that timing difference remains unresolved. We have not checked Meal Monster’s widget capacity on the shipping version.

Choose sharing voluntarily, review the recipient and visibility settings in the current app, and check access terms before subscribing. Weight here means actual body weight; it does not cover every achievement, message, or number a person could choose to post.

Existing work and what this adds

Kalo already explains social calorie-tracker categories, and a couples and families comparison already exists. This finder adds explicit field-level source links and leaves unanswered questions visible. It does not establish nutritional accuracy or promise better results.
