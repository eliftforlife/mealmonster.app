What to check on your phone

  • Check whether one spoken sentence can contain multiple foods.
  • Review the transcript separately from the calorie estimate.
  • Distinguish Siri shortcuts and database dictation from arbitrary meal descriptions.
  • A text benchmark does not establish microphone or speech-recognition performance.
