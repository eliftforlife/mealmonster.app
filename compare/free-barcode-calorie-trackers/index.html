What to check on your phone

  • Use the same packaged product and check the matched serving size.
  • Confirm that a barcode opens nutrition data you can save, rather than an upgrade screen.
  • Database coverage varies; one successful scan does not prove every product is covered.
  • Photographing a nutrition label is a separate capability from scanning a barcode.
