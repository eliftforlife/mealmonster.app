What to check on your phone

  • Check whether typing parses an entire meal or only searches the database.
  • Check that sauces, sides, and cooking fats survive the first estimate.
  • Keep portions explicit when you know them; check raw versus cooked weights.
  • Look for editing controls before saving, and check the plan required.
