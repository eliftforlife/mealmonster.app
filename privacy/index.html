Privacy Policy

Meal Monster provides food logging, nutrition estimates, progress tracking, and related account features. This policy explains what information Meal Monster collects, why it is used, when it is disclosed, how long it is kept, and the choices available to users.

Contact: support@mealmonster.app

Information collected

Meal Monster may collect:

  • Account information, including email address, authentication identifiers, and account settings.
  • Nutrition and wellness information, including age or birth date, height, weight, weight goals, calorie and nutrient targets, activity or workout information, appetite and routine answers, meal logs, saved foods, and weigh-ins.
  • Content submitted for features, including meal descriptions, food photos, voice recordings or transcripts, barcode scans, and profile photos.
  • Purchase information, including subscription status, product, trial, renewal, and expiration information received from app stores and RevenueCat. Meal Monster does not receive full payment-card details from the app stores.
  • Device, notification, usage, and diagnostic information needed to deliver features, prevent abuse, enforce limits, troubleshoot failures, and protect accounts.

Meal Monster receives information directly from users, from their devices when permission is granted, from app stores and service providers, and from public food databases used for barcode and nutrient information.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts; provide food logging, nutrition estimates, progress, reminders, and requested features; personalize calorie and nutrient targets; process subscriptions; respond to support, export, and deletion requests; prevent fraud and abuse; maintain reliability; comply with legal obligations; and enforce applicable terms.

Meal Monster does not sell personal information. Meal Monster does not use health or fitness information for advertising or allow it to be used for advertising. Meal Monster does not use AI submissions to train Meal Monster models without a separate, newly approved consent process.

AI processing

AI food logging is optional. Before the first AI request, Meal Monster asks for affirmative consent to send the specific meal text, voice recording or transcript, or food photo the user chooses to submit through Meal Monster servers to OpenAI. OpenAI processes that content to provide the requested transcription, classification, or nutrition estimate.

Consent is recorded with the notice version, named processor, processing scope, and acceptance or revocation time. A user may turn off AI data sharing in the Privacy screen. After it is turned off, AI features remain unavailable until the user affirmatively enables them again. Manual food logging and barcode scanning remain available.

AI outputs and nutrition values are estimates and may be wrong or incomplete. Users can review or correct an estimate before saving it.

Service providers and disclosures

Meal Monster uses Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage, and server functions; OpenAI for user-enabled AI features; RevenueCat and Apple or Google app stores for subscriptions; Expo for notifications and application infrastructure; Open Food Facts for barcode information; and USDA FoodData Central for nutrient information.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users or the service, investigate abuse or security incidents, complete a corporate transaction subject to appropriate safeguards, or act on a user's direction. Providers are expected to process information only for contracted purposes and protect it appropriately.

Health and fitness information

Meal Monster treats nutrition, weight, goal, food-log, activity, and related information as sensitive. Apple Health information is accessed only after platform permission and is used to provide user-requested health-management features. Health and fitness information is not used for marketing, advertising, or unrelated data mining.

Storage, retention, and security

Account, profile, nutrition, and saved preference data is generally retained while an account is active. Raw food photos and voice recordings are processed for the requested feature and are not retained by Meal Monster by default. A saved meal may retain attachment metadata or a device-local image reference selected by the user. Profile photos are stored in a private bucket and displayed using short-lived signed links.

Information is deleted or deidentified when no longer reasonably needed, subject to backup cycles and limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, billing, legal, dispute, or compliance purposes. Meal Monster uses access controls, encrypted transport, owner-scoped database policies, secure mobile-session storage, server-held credentials, usage limits, and monitoring intended to protect information. No security method is perfect.

User choices and rights

Users can review and update supported profile information; turn optional permissions off; turn AI data sharing off; export Meal Monster account data; cancel subscriptions through the app-store account used for purchase; and permanently delete the Meal Monster account and associated app data in the app.

Deleting a Meal Monster account does not cancel an app-store subscription. Some billing, security, fraud-prevention, legal, or compliance records may be retained when required.

Depending on where a user lives, additional rights may include access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or appeal of a denied request. Requests may be sent to support@mealmonster.app. Meal Monster will authenticate requests before acting on account information.

Children

Meal Monster is intended only for adults age 18 and older. It is not directed to children, and Meal Monster does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security incidents

Meal Monster maintains an incident-response process. If a breach or unauthorized disclosure requires notice, Meal Monster will notify affected users, regulators, and others as required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be identified by a new effective date and, when required, a clear in-app notice or renewed consent. Information will not be repurposed in a materially different way without notice and consent when required.
