How to Log Meals Faster

By Eli, founder of Meal Monster · former personal trainer with a nutrition certification · Updated 2026-10-19

The short answer

The fastest log is the one you don't have to search for. Describe the whole meal in one sentence, add portions you know, and reuse the meals you eat often.

Five habits that make logging quick

  1. One sentence per meal. "Two eggs, toast and a banana" logs the whole breakfast at once.
  2. Say it instead of typing. Voice logging is faster when your hands are full.
  3. Snap it when it's hard to describe. A photo works for a plate in front of you, but check the portion it estimates.
  4. Scan packaged food. A supported barcode skips the guessing when product data is available.
  5. Log right after you eat. It takes seconds while you remember, and minutes when you're reconstructing a day.

Why it matters

In our analysis of 1,000 weight loss transformations, tracking food was the single most common eating change (25% of stories). The easier logging is, the more likely you are to keep doing it.

Questions

What is the fastest way to log food?

Describing the whole meal in one sentence, by typing or voice, instead of searching for each ingredient. Reusing meals you eat often helps too.

Are photo calorie estimates accurate?

They're estimates. A photo can't guarantee every ingredient or portion, so it's worth checking and editing the result.

Does Meal Monster work with barcodes?

Yes, for supported packaged foods when usable product data is available. Coverage varies.
