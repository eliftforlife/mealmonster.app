How it works

We estimate your maintenance calories, then subtract the deficit you pick. The result never goes below 1,200 calories for women or 1,500 for men. If your weight is already low for your height, we show maintenance instead of a deficit.

Questions

What is a calorie deficit?

It means taking in fewer calories than your body uses over time.

How big should a calorie deficit be?

Many guidelines point to about 250 to 500 calories a day for adults. A smaller deficit is usually easier to keep up, and results vary from person to person.

Why does the calculator stop at a minimum?

Eating very little makes it hard to get enough nutrients. Going below the minimum is something to do with a doctor.

Track it without the math.

Type, say or snap what you ate, and Meal Monster estimates the calories and protein. Every estimate is editable.
