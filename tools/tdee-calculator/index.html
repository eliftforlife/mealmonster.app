How it works

We estimate your BMR with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then multiply by an activity factor:

  • Mostly sitting: 1.2
  • Light: 1.375
  • Moderate: 1.55
  • Very active: 1.725

Treat the result as a starting point. If your weight trends up or down over 2 to 3 weeks, adjust from there.

Questions

What does TDEE stand for?

Total daily energy expenditure: everything you burn in a day, including rest, digestion, movement and exercise.

Are maintenance calories the same as TDEE?

Yes. Maintenance calories are the calories that keep your weight about the same, which is your TDEE.

How accurate is a TDEE calculator?

It gives an estimate. Your real number depends on your body, your job and how you move, so your actual weight trend over a few weeks is the better guide.
