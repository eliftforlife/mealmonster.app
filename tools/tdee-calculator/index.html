How it works

We estimate your BMR with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then multiply by an activity factor:

  • Mostly sitting: 1.2
  • Light (on your feet, or 1 to 3 workouts a week): 1.375
  • Moderate (3 to 5 workouts a week): 1.55
  • Very active (6 to 7 hard workouts a week): 1.725

Treat the result as a starting point. If your weight trends up or down over 2 to 3 weeks, adjust from there. Want the number to eat to keep your weight, at every activity level? Use the maintenance calorie calculator.

Questions

What does TDEE stand for?

Total daily energy expenditure: everything you burn in a day, including rest, digestion, movement and exercise.

Are maintenance calories the same as TDEE?

Yes. Maintenance calories are the calories that keep your weight about the same, which is your TDEE. The maintenance calorie calculator shows it for every activity level.

How accurate is a TDEE calculator?

It gives an estimate. Your real number depends on your body, your job and how you move, so your actual weight trend over a few weeks is the better guide.
