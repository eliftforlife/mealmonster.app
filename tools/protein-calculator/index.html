How it works

Questions

How much protein do I need a day?

The basic RDA is 0.8 g per kg of body weight. People who train regularly often aim for about 1.2 to 2.0 g per kg.

Can I eat more protein than the RDA?

For most healthy adults, higher intakes are common. If you have kidney disease, talk to your doctor first.
