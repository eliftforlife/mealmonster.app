How it works

We estimate your calories, then set protein at 1.6 g per kg, fat at about 28% of calories, and carbs as the rest. It's a starting point. The best split is one you can keep up.

Questions

What are macros?

Macronutrients are protein, carbohydrates and fat, the three main sources of calories in food.

How are calories worked out from macros?

Protein and carbs have about 4 calories per gram, and fat has about 9.

Track it without the math.

Type, say or snap what you ate, and Meal Monster estimates the calories and protein. Every estimate is editable.
