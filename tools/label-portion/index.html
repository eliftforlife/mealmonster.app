More help and sources

What is a serving size?

It’s the amount beside “Serving size” on the package. The calories on the label are for that amount.

Why does cooking matter?

Food can gain or lose water when cooked. Use a label that matches how you measured your food.

Is the answer exact?

No. Labels use rounded numbers. Use your own package label, and include any oil or other food you added.

Read the FDA guide to food labels. The cookie example uses made-up numbers to show how this works. The turkey example uses the Butterball 85/15 ground turkey label: 230 calories per 4 ounces; checked October 28, 2026. Calculations stay in this browser. They aren’t saved or sent to a food service.
