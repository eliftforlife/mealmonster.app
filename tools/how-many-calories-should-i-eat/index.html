Questions

How many calories should I eat a day?

It depends on your height, weight, age, sex and activity. The 2,000 calories on nutrition labels is a general reference, not a personal target.

Is 2,000 calories a day right for me?

It might be close, but many adults need more or fewer. An estimate based on your own numbers is a better starting point.
